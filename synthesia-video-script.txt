I found a bird in the street yesterday. Just sitting there. Not moving. Wings kind of drooped.

I could tell it was hurt or exhausted. So I grabbed some water, some birdseed. Sat down next to it.

Fed it. Gave it water. Just stayed there. Waited.

After maybe twenty minutes, something changed. The bird started moving again. Getting stronger. I could feel it.

So I opened my hands and let it go. Watched it fly up.

And then it came back. Landed right on my shoulder. Like it knew.

That moment right there? That's everything you need to know.
